Rates

Private Charter Rates:

Each private Key Sailing experience can be designed personally.

Our private charter pricing for the whole yacht including the captain, snacks, and everything but voluntary gratituity is $200 an hour for parties of six or fewer. Do you have a special occassion or more than six guests? Call Jan at 941-346-7245 for options and ideas to meet the needs of your group.

Because we never take a deposit, we don't need a final guest count until the day of the sail, and because all charters are private charters that you design yourself, you simply pay accordingly that day.

Our most popular package is three hours, but we also offer two hours, four hours, and even half hour increments such as 2 1/2 and 3 1/2 hours, to make budgeting easier for you.

How do I make a reservation?

Fill out this request form or call or text 941-346-7245 (SAIL) anytime, 24/7. You are not confirmed until you hear back from us. We will customize the details to suit your needs.

Reservation & Payment

We never require a deposit, and reserve with just your name and phone number. Call us or email us to reserve your personalized cruise today.

We accept cash, checks, Venmo, Zelle, and even credit cards after your charter to keep things easy and flexible in case of changing weather or in case your needs change. Gratuities are graciously accepted and help us help others. Advance payments for suprises or corporate charters are completely refundable.

What's Included

  • Music or the quiet of the lapping waves
  • Comfortable life jackets for your children
  • Pillows, cushions, and back rests
  • Snuggies and blankets to enjoy a winter sunset
  • Snacks and soft drinks, bottled water
  • Photos, if you wish
  • Sailing lessons - also optional
  • Chocolates for everyone!

We are also happy to offer local ideas to cater lunch or dinner for your special anniversary celebration. Each charter is customized for your needs. Let us know how many guests you have, and how we can personalize your sail.

People often also ask about adult beverages. We do have bottle openers and glasses. You are welcome to bring white wine (red can be a bit messy), champagne or beer. (Hard liquor is not appropriate for a sailing charter.) The Key Breeze is also a "smoke free" vessel.

Packages

Choose the Package which best accommodates your sailing desires.

Dolphin sightings are common (but never promised) on all cruises.

TWO HOUR BAY CHARTERS are great for families in the morning, for a taste of true sailing in the afternoon, or for a spectacular sunset cruise at the edge of the Gulf of Mexico! Perfect for wildlife sightings and for the budget. We usually have a waiting list, so please book your sailing choice as early as possible!

THREE HOUR DAY OR SUNSET CHARTERS are our most popular choice to give you that extra hour in the bay or gulf, or as many guests prefer, to head under the John Ringling Causeway toward the Ringling Mansion in the North Bay. You and the captain will plan your route right from the yacht on the day of sail!

FOUR HOUR CHARTERS give you enough time for the option of true Gulf sailing. In addition, for those who choose to take the helm, a sailing certificate is provided.
